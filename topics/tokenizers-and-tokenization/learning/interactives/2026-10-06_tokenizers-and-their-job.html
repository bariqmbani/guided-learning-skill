Tokenizers and Their Job

A language model works with a sequence of token IDs. A tokenizer turns the text you give it into manageable pieces, then looks up an ID for each piece.

1 · Input

Text

The sentence you type.

2 · Tokenizer

Token pieces

Text is divided into pieces. A piece may be a word, part of a word, punctuation, or a character.

3 · Vocabulary lookup

Token IDs

Each piece is matched to an integer in that tokenizer’s vocabulary.

4 · Model input

Embeddings

The model uses each ID to look up a learned vector, then processes the vector sequence.

What to notice
  • Switch from word-like pieces to characters. How much longer does the sequence become?
  • Try a long or uncommon word. Splitting it into pieces can help a model handle words it has not seen as one whole vocabulary item, though it may use more tokens.
  • Token IDs are labels used for lookup. ID 101 does not mean “first,” “important,” or any particular word by itself.
