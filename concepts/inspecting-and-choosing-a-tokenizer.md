# Inspecting and Choosing a Tokenizer

## Core claim
Choosing a tokenizer means checking how it segments the text that matters, including ordinary prose, names, code, multilingual text, and unusual symbols. Useful comparisons include token counts, reversibility, special-token behavior, and compatibility with the intended model; the best choice depends on the task and data.
