# Tokenizer Training and Vocabulary Size

## Core claim
Tokenizer training learns a vocabulary and the rules or scores used to segment text from a chosen corpus. Vocabulary size is a tradeoff: larger vocabularies can encode common strings in fewer tokens but require more model parameters and may allocate capacity poorly to rare or underrepresented text.
