# WordPiece Tokenization

## Core claim
WordPiece is a subword tokenization method associated with models such as BERT. It chooses and applies vocabulary pieces according to a learned scoring or likelihood-based procedure, so its behavior resembles BPE at a high level but does not use the same simple frequency-merge rule.
