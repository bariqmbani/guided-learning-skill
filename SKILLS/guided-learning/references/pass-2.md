# Pass 2 — Working Understanding

Read only for the current Working Understanding pass. The goal is to explain
the mechanism and connect it to at least two other concepts.

## Explain

Build on Pass 1 intuition, adapting depth to the outcome and time available:

- Trace an algorithm, method, or process on a concrete, realistic example.
- Evaluate the evidence behind its claims. For an empirical study, inspect the
  design, sample size, effect size, and statistical tests when available; explain
  what these support instead of merely listing the findings.
- Identify boundary conditions and where the mechanism or evidence fails.
- Compare related concepts already covered: what each adds, where they agree,
  and where they conflict.
- Locate a specific application in the learner's project, research, or practice.

Do not manufacture study details or treat missing evidence as established fact.
Follow shared teaching for attempts and feedback throughout the walkthrough.

## Check comprehension

Choose one format below for the current domain; do not repeat the previous
session's `check_format`. Retain its ID in the execution log and adapt wording
to the actual concept and language while retaining the underlying task. Let the
learner explore a relevant interactive first, then ask and wait. Address gaps
before advancing.

| Shared ID | Prompt and eligibility |
| --- | --- |
| `two-concept-bridge` | How does this connect to [previous concept]? What does one provide that the other doesn't? |
| `devils-advocate` | I think [opposing claim]. Convince me I'm wrong using this concept. |
| `evidence-check` | Research or self-study: What's the strongest evidence for this claim, and its biggest limitation? |
| `design-decision` | Research or professional: Where does this change your system design, and how? |
| `predict-failure` | Under what conditions would this fail? Research: give a concrete domain example; professional: consider a real deployment. |

| Task | Research | Professional | Self-study |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Explain the mechanism | `advisor-pitch`: Explain the mechanism to your advisor. | `manager-briefing`: Write a one-paragraph briefing for your manager. | `teach-a-friend`: Explain the mechanism to someone smart but unfamiliar. |

Additional domain-specific formats:

- **Professional — `cost-benefit`:** What's the cost of implementing this versus not implementing it?
- **Self-study — `build-something`:** In a project using this, what would you implement first?
- **Self-study — `edge-case`:** What's the most extreme scenario where this still applies?

## Connections

Choose a related concept from an earlier cluster and ask how this one changes
or strengthens the learner's understanding of it. Seek at least two supported
connections across the lesson; supplied connections count as assisted work.
