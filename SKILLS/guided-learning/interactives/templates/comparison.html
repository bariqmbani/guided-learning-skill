04 · Explain the difference

Set the rate to 10% and inspect rounds 1 and 2. What quantity is the percentage applied to in each model? Explain the first round in which the totals differ.

Reveal a worked check

With P = 100, both reach 110 after round 1. In round 2, fixed addition adds another 10, reaching 120. Compound growth adds 10% of 110, or 11, reaching 121.

Transfer the idea

A culture gains 10 cells each hour in one model and 10% of its current cells each hour in another. What changes if both begin with 200 cells? Which assumption would fail as space runs out?

Model limits. Units are arbitrary and divisible. Rates are constant, growth is applied once per round, and there are no losses, resource limits, or outside contributions. These formulas illustrate arithmetic and geometric growth; they are not forecasts of a real system.
