    03 · Explain it in your own words

    A worked answer

    The mean adds every value, so one 130-minute ticket is spread across all nine and lifts the mean well above what a typical ticket costs. The median ignores how far the extreme value sits and reports the middle ticket, so it describes the typical case better. For staffing, report the median for the typical load and the range or the longest case separately, because the rare long ticket is exactly what overruns a shift.

    04 · Transfer the idea

    A shop reports a mean basket of 42 units while most baskets are near 15. What kind of value must be in that dataset, and which statistic would you ask for instead?

    Make a harder case

    Change one number in the dataset so that the mean and the median agree. Which number did you change, and what does that tell you about when the mean is safe to quote?

    Model limits. The nine durations are illustrative, not measured data. The lesson is about which statistic answers which question; it is not a claim about real support workloads. The mode is reported here because one value repeats, which is not true of most continuous data.
