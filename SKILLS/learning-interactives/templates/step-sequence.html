The bar spans the remaining candidates. A copy of the midpoint value travels to the comparison below; the array stays unchanged. Watch low and high move inward after each comparison. Pause freezes the motion. Next shows an operation; Back visibly undoes it. Speed changes both directions.

How the midpoint is calculated

  1. Set low and high to the ends.
  2. Compare the middle value.
  3. If too small, move low right.
  4. If too large, move high left.
  5. If equal, return the index.

Transfer the idea

Trace a search for 15 on paper. After comparing 16, low becomes 4 and high becomes 3. Why does low > high mean the target is absent?

Check your trace

Compare 12, then 23, then 16. The remaining range becomes empty. Since each exclusion preserved every possible match, there is no 15 in the array.

Model limits. This trace uses a fixed, sorted array of distinct numbers and an existing target. Binary search requires sorted data. For duplicate values, this version can find a match without guaranteeing the first match. Seven visual steps represent three comparisons, not seven comparisons.
