03 · Explain the bend

The angle changes equally each second. Why does height change slowly near the top and bottom, but quickly as the point passes the center’s height?

Check your explanation

Near the top and bottom, most of the motion is sideways. Near the center’s height, most of it is vertical. Equal changes in angle do not make equal changes in height.

Transfer the idea

If the radius doubled and the period stayed 8 seconds, what would change in the wave?

Check your answer

The heights would double to range from −2 to +2, but the quarter-turns would still occur at 2, 4, 6, and 8 seconds. Radius sets amplitude; period sets the timing.

Model limits. This is an ideal point with unit radius and constant angular speed. The wave is a height-versus-time graph; it is not a second object moving through space, and this animation does not model forces or energy.
