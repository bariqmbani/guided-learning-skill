# Glossary

Terms are added alphabetically during learning sessions, with definitions and links to their concept notes.

## B
- **Byte** — A unit of digital data; text encodings represent characters as one or more bytes. See [[byte-level-tokenization]].

## M
- **Merge** — In BPE, a learned rule that combines neighboring symbols or pieces into a larger token. See [[byte-pair-encoding]].

## N
- **Normalization** — A consistent transformation of input text before tokenization, such as handling equivalent forms. See [[normalization-and-pre-tokenization]].

## P
- **Pre-tokenization** — An initial split into text chunks before a tokenizer applies its main segmentation method. See [[normalization-and-pre-tokenization]].

## S
- **Subword** — A text piece smaller than a word that can be combined with other pieces to represent words. See [[token-granularity]].

## T
- **Token** — A discrete text unit produced by a tokenizer and represented by an ID for a language model. See [[tokenizers-and-their-job]].
- **Token ID** — The integer assigned to a token in a particular vocabulary. See [[vocabularies-token-ids-and-embeddings]].

## V
- **Vocabulary** — The set of token strings and their IDs known to a tokenizer. See [[vocabularies-token-ids-and-embeddings]].
